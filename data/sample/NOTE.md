# About `sample_features.csv`

This is a randomly sampled subset of trading days (290 of the 713 rows in
the full engineered feature table) for demonstration purposes, drawn with a
fixed seed and scattered across the complete 2023-2025 date range — not the
most recent data. The complete feature table, the raw snapshot history it's
derived from (2,114 files), and the trained production model are
proprietary and not published in this repository.
